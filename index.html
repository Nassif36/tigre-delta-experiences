GOOD TO KNOW

Before you set off.

A little local context for planning your visit.

What is the Tigre Delta?+

It is a network of rivers, streams and islands in the lower Paraná Delta, near the city of Tigre in Buenos Aires Province, Argentina.

How do I get to Tigre?+

Tigre can be reached from Buenos Aires by train or car. We’ll arrange the meeting point when you book.

How does booking work?+

When WhatsApp inquiries are available, the booking button opens a message to us. Availability and details are confirmed directly.
