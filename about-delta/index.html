A WORLD SHAPED BY WATER

Meet the Tigre Delta.

A landscape of rivers, islands and everyday life, just beyond Buenos Aires and a world away from the city’s rhythm.

THE WATERWAYS

Where the river becomes a way of life.

The Tigre Delta is part of the lower Paraná Delta: an intricate landscape of rivers, smaller streams and islands in Buenos Aires Province. Water is its defining feature and a natural way to move through it.

From Tigre, boats follow channels past homes, docks and vegetation. Every route offers a different perspective on this changing wetland landscape.

THE ISLANDS

A community connected by water.

The islands are not just scenery. They are home to communities whose daily routines are closely connected to the river. Docks and boats link homes, local businesses and services.

Exploring with a local perspective can help visitors understand this living culture while respecting the people who call the Delta home.

THE NATURAL LANDSCAPE

A place to slow down and notice.

The Delta’s wetlands support a variety of plants and wildlife. Trees frame the waterways, while changing water levels and weather shape the landscape.

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. Travel thoughtfully, follow local guidance and leave the places you visit as you found them.
